Why attend

Strategy

Plan the year ahead

Set ASAAL's 2026–2027 priorities — endorsements, legislative pushes, voter-registration drives, and new chapter launches.

Speakers

Elected officials & allies

Hear directly from members of Congress, state legislators, union presidents, and coalition leaders we've worked with for years.

Awards

Recognition gala

An evening to honor members whose work made the year's wins possible. Black-tie. By invitation.

Training

Organizer workshops

Hands-on training in doorknocking, phone-banking, voter outreach, and chapter leadership.

Community

Cultural programs

Music, performance, and food from across the South Asian diaspora — Afghan, Bangladeshi, Bhutanese, Indian, Maldivian, Nepalese, Pakistani, Sri Lankan.

Network

Cross-chapter meetups

Connect with organizers from Queens to Houston to LA. Build relationships that carry the work forward all year.
