§ 03 — Artwork requirements

What to send us.

№ 01

File format

High-resolution PDF (preferred) or 300dpi PNG / TIFF. Fonts embedded or outlined. Vector logos placed at 100% scale or larger.

№ 02

Color

CMYK only. Convert spot/Pantone colors before delivery. Rich black = C40 / M30 / Y30 / K100 for solid blacks.

№ 03

Bleed & safety

Add 0.125 in bleed beyond the trim on full-bleed sizes. Keep critical text 0.25 in inside the trim line.

№ 04

Submission

Upload via the application form, or email journal@asaal.org. We reply within 48 hours with proof and confirmation.
