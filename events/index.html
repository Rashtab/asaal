§ 06 — Six months ahead

The road from here to November.

A snapshot of the season's flagship moments. Scroll horizontally on narrow screens.

  1. JUN

    Youth Leadership Workshop

    Sat, June 13 · Atlanta, GA

    Full-day workshop for high-school and college students — civic literacy, public speaking, Q&A with state legislators.

  2. JUL
    Flagship

    18th National Convention

    Jul 18 – 20 · Atlantic City, NJ

    The largest annual gathering of South Asian American labor in the United States.

    Convention site →
  3. AUG

    Chapter Leadership Summit

    Sat, August 8 · Virtual

    Half-day training for chapter officers — governance, budgeting, member engagement, 2026–27 strategy.

  4. SEP

    Labor Day Mobilization

    Fri, September 4 · NYC

    ASAAL marches with the NY State AFL-CIO. Multi-borough staging. Buses from every NYC chapter.

  5. OCT

    Women's National Leadership Forum

    Oct 4 – 5 · Washington, DC

    Two-day national forum bringing together 200+ women members. Speakers from Congress, labor, academia.

  6. NOV

    Community Service Month

    All November · 20 chapters

    Every chapter runs at least one volunteer project. Food drives, neighborhood cleanups, supply distributions.
