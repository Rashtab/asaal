§ Executive Directory · 2025–2026

National
Executive Council.

The complete governing body of ASAAL — the officers, vice presidents, trustees, and standing committees that lead the federation between Conventions.

Executive Officers (11) Vice Presidents (39) Committees Trustees (7) Women's Committee (31) Youth Committee (22)
§ 01

Executive Officers

11 members · elected at Convention

The Founder & President Emeritus, the National President, and nine directors who carry day-to-day operational responsibility for the federation.

§ 02

Vice Presidents

39 members

Cross-chapter and cross-professional leadership — representing regional chapters, professional sectors (medical, legal, academic), and constituencies across the South Asian diaspora.

    § National Committees

    Three standing committees.

    Beyond the elected officers, three standing committees extend ASAAL's reach into fiduciary oversight, women's leadership, and the next generation.

    § 03

    Board of Trustees

    7 trustees

    Fiduciary oversight, strategic guidance, and institutional memory. The Trustees include senior leaders, scholars, and the Founder & current National President — anchoring continuity across decades of organizing.

      § 04

      Women's Committee

      31 members · Chair: Adan Islam

      Empowering South Asian American women in labor, politics, and civic life — and developing the next generation of women leaders within ASAAL and beyond.

        § 05

        Youth Committee

        22 members · 5 scholarship winners

        High-school and college-age members building skills, scholarship, and a pipeline into civic leadership. Scholarship winners are marked with the Scholar badge — recognized at the 18th Annual Convention.

          ASAAL Youth Scholarship Program

          Scholarships are announced annually at the National Convention. The 2025 winners join the Youth Committee's permanent honor roll.
