Getting there

Travel from anywhere in the country.

By Air

Atlantic City Int'l (ACY) — 20 min. Philadelphia Int'l (PHL) — 60 min. Newark Liberty (EWR) — 2 hrs.

By Bus

NJ Transit bus from NYC Port Authority — 2.5 hrs direct. Greyhound and Megabus also serve AC.

By Car

Atlantic City Expressway exit 1. Self-parking and valet available at the Sheraton.

By Train

NJ Transit Atlantic City Line from 30th St Station, Philadelphia — 90 min. AC Rail Terminal is 5 min from the Sheraton.

Hotel FAQ

Things to know before you book.

When is the ASAAL block deadline?+

June 1, 2026. After that, rooms revert to public rates and availability isn't guaranteed.

Cancellation policy?+

Free cancellation up to 72 hours before check-in. After that, one night's rate is charged.

Roommate matching?+

Yes — ASAAL can match you with another attendee from your chapter to share a double-queen room and split costs. Contact convention@asaal.org.

Accessibility?+

The Sheraton is ADA-compliant. Accessible rooms can be requested at booking. The convention venue and gala ballroom are step-free.
