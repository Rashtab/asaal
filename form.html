FAQ

Common questions about membership.

Who can join ASAAL?+

Membership is open to all South Asian workers (Afghan, Bangladeshi, Bhutanese, Indian, Maldivian, Nepalese, Pakistani, Sri Lankani origin), union members, community activists, leaders, professionals, self-employed individuals, and small business owners — anyone who believes in the ASAAL movement.

How much are membership dues?+

Annual dues are paid through our secure portal at pay.asaal.org. The portal lists current dues tiers, including reduced-rate options for students and retirees.

Do I need to be in a union to join?+

No. ASAAL members include union and non-union workers, community activists, professionals, and small business owners. If you're not in a union and would like to be, ASAAL can help you connect with one in your industry.

What does membership get me?+

Membership gets you a vote in chapter and national leadership elections, an invitation to the annual convention, voter-mobilization toolkits, regular policy briefings, and access to ASAAL's network across 20 chapters and 11 states.

I have a question that's not here.+

Email info@asaal.org or use the contact form on the homepage. We typically respond within 2 business days.
